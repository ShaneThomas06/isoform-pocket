# AlphaFold 3 matched-control comparison

## Main result

Both full-length complexes have failed global interface confidence: mean ipTM is 0.17 for RAC1B-p120 and 0.17 for RAC1-p120. Therefore, neither displayed binding pose is reliable.

Despite that limitation, the residue-level contact probabilities differ sharply. RAC1B residues 76-81 at the start of the unique insertion have mean maximum p120 contact probabilities of 0.50-0.67. RAC1 has no residue pair with contact probability >=0.50 in any of its five models. The 13 RAC1B-p120 pairs above 0.50 recur in all five diffusion models.

The implicated p120-4A residues are 69, 72, 107, 110, 111, 114, 151, 154 and 155, corresponding to canonical CTNND1 residues 392, 395, 430, 433, 434, 437, 474, 477 and 478.

## Interpretation

This is a differential hypothesis signal, not a solved complex. The result supports focused modeling of the p120 armadillo-repeat region containing canonical residues 392-478, followed by additional seeds and experimental mutation proposals.
